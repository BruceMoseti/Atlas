What this does and does not show
  Every number above comes from the SQLite database the run produced,
  read after every process had exited. The attempt counts are physical
  executions, not logical jobs: under at-least-once semantics a job that
  was retried really did run more than once, and Atlas does not pretend
  otherwise. What it does claim is that no accepted job disappeared, no
  terminal state was ever revisited, no worker was oversubscribed, and no
  stale attempt decided a job's outcome.
